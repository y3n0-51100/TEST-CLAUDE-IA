# Shelfie — charte graphique et prompts de génération

## 1. Identité (à respecter dans tous les visuels)

- **Promesse** : « Range. Trie. Savoure. » — le plaisir satisfaisant de tout remettre en ordre.
- **Ton** : joyeux, doux, léger, jamais agressif. Phrases courtes. Emojis : 🤤 ✨ 🎁 😳.
- **Univers** : bonbons gélatineux brillants en 3D posés sur des étagères, ambiance cosy et colorée.
- **Mascotte** : **Jelly**, un cube gélatineux rose bonbon, brillant, avec deux grands yeux ronds et un petit sourire. Pas de bras ni de jambes.
- **Handle** : `@shelfie.game`

### Palette
| Rôle | Couleur |
|---|---|
| Fond dégradé | `#FFD6F0` → `#D9C7FF` → `#BFE3FF` |
| Accent principal (rose bonbon) | `#FF4FA3` (ombre `#C42A78`) |
| Encre / texte | `#4A2C6B` |
| Étagère | `#F4A6D7` |
| Objets | rouge `#FF6B6B`, bleu `#4DABF7`, vert `#51CF66`, jaune `#FCC419`, violet `#CC5DE8`, orange `#FF922B` |
| Neon (thème nuit) | fond `#140A33`, violet `#B266FF`, cyan `#00D5FF` |
| Cozy (thème bois) | crème `#F6DCC0`, bois `#C79569`, brique `#E76F51` |

### Typographie
Titres : **Baloo 2 ExtraBold** ou **Nunito Black** (arrondies, épaisses). Logo en blanc avec ombre rose `#C42A78` décalée vers le bas (effet 3D). Texte courant : Nunito Bold.

### Règles
- Toujours des formes **arrondies** (rayon ≥ 40 %), jamais d'angles vifs.
- Toujours un **reflet blanc brillant** en haut à gauche des objets et une **tranche plus foncée** dessous (effet bonbon 3D).
- Un seul élément héros par visuel, fond simple, beaucoup d'air.
- Aucun texte long sur les visuels : 4 mots maximum.

## 2. Prompts image (GPT Image / Midjourney / Ideogram / Canva)

Ajoute en fin de prompt, selon l'outil : `--ar 1:1` (Midjourney) ou précise le format dans la phrase.

**Suffixe de style commun (à coller à la fin de chaque prompt)** :
> glossy jelly candy 3D render, soft studio lighting, glossy white highlight top-left, darker thick base edge, rounded shapes, pastel pink-lilac-blue gradient background, clean minimal composition, high-end mobile game key art, no text unless specified

### Logo (wordmark)
> Logo wordmark reading "Shelfie" in chunky rounded extra-bold letters, white with a thick 3D candy-pink (#C42A78) extrusion and soft drop shadow, tiny glossy jelly cubes (red circle, blue square, yellow star) sitting on the top edge of the letters like on a shelf, centered on a soft pink-lilac gradient background, vector-clean mobile game logo.

### Icône d'app (variante si tu veux remplacer celle fournie)
> Mobile app icon, 1024x1024, three glossy jelly candy cubes side by side (red with white circle, blue with white square, yellow with white star) on a pink candy shelf, pastel pink to lilac to sky-blue gradient background, centered, large and bold, subtle depth, no text, rounded-square safe zone.

### Mascotte Jelly (feuille de personnage)
> Character sheet of "Jelly", a cute glossy pink jelly cube mascot with two big round shiny eyes and a tiny smile, no arms, no legs, soft translucent candy material, white highlight top-left. Poses: idle, happy jump, surprised, sleepy, celebrating with confetti, thinking. Neutral soft lilac background, 3D render, consistent character, front and three-quarter view.

### Avatar TikTok / Instagram (rond)
> Square profile picture, close-up of Jelly the pink glossy jelly cube mascot smiling, centered, big and simple, pastel lilac background with a soft glow, bold shapes readable at 100 pixels, no text.

### Affiche 1 — « Satisfaisant » (portrait 4:5)
> Poster, a perfectly sorted wooden-pink shelf with rows of glossy jelly candy cubes in matching colors, one cube floating mid-air about to land with a small sparkle burst, big empty space at the top for a headline, pastel pink and lilac gradient, cozy and joyful, mobile game advertising key art.

### Affiche 2 — « Défi niveau 25 » (portrait 4:5)
> Poster, dark neon arcade mood, many glass-like glowing cubes in purple, cyan, green and orange sorted on neon-edged shelves, one shelf almost solved with a question mark glowing above, deep violet background with a perspective grid floor, dramatic but friendly, mobile puzzle game key art.

### Affiche 3 — « Cozy Shop » (portrait 4:5)
> Poster, warm cozy shop interior with wooden shelves lined with glass jars in candy colors, soft golden light from the top, striped cream wallpaper, tiny floating sparkles, calm and satisfying atmosphere, mobile puzzle game key art.

### Visuel de lancement (carré 1:1)
> Celebration scene, Jelly the pink jelly cube mascot jumping with confetti and colorful jelly cubes falling around, shelf at the bottom, bright pastel gradient background, big empty area at the top for text "Disponible maintenant", joyful launch announcement.

### Couverture de story / à la une Instagram (cercle, à décliner ×4)
> Minimal round icon, one glossy jelly cube on a pastel pink background, centered, simple bold shape, variants: a star cube, a heart cube, a gift box cube, a trophy cube. No text.

### Fond de vidéo TikTok (9:16)
> Vertical 9:16 abstract background, soft pastel pink-lilac-blue gradient with floating blurred glossy jelly cubes and sparkles, large clean center area, no text, mobile video backdrop.

### Pack de stickers (6 petits visuels)
> Sticker sheet of Jelly the pink jelly cube mascot, six expressions with thick white outline: happy, shocked, proud, sleepy, cheering, heart eyes. Glossy candy 3D, transparent-friendly white border, consistent character.

## 3. Prompts vidéo (Runway / Kling / Seedance)

À partir d'une image de la mascotte ou d'une capture du jeu.

**Intro de 3 s (logo)**
> Static camera, pastel gradient background, glossy jelly cubes drop one by one onto a candy-pink shelf with a squash-and-stretch bounce, sparkles burst, then the white 3D wordmark "Shelfie" pops in with a bounce. Cheerful, satisfying, smooth 3D animation, 9:16.

**Boucle satisfaisante (6 s)**
> Top-down tilt of a cozy shelf, glossy jelly cubes sorting themselves by color in sequence with soft bounce and tiny sparkle on each landing, calm rhythm, seamless loop, 9:16.

**Fin de vidéo (2 s)**
> Jelly the pink jelly cube mascot jumps into frame, waves, confetti falls, soft pastel background, empty top area for a call-to-action text, 9:16.
